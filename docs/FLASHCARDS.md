# StudyMind — Interactive Flashcards (Quizlet-Style)

StudyMind's flashcard system uses visual active-recall principles and a spaced-repetition state machine.

---

## 1. Pedagogical Deck Modes

- **Definitions & Terminology**: Builds vocabulary and technical definitions.
- **Core Conceptual Explanations**: Deep mechanism and principle breakdowns.
- **Formulas, Rules & Math**: Mathematical models, algorithm complexities, equations.
- **High-Yield Exam Prep**: Frequent exam questions and high-yield facts.
- **Mixed Comprehensive Review**: A balanced mix of all formats.

---

## 2. Interactive 3D Card UX

- **Perspective Flip**: Single click/tap flips between Front (Prompt / Concept) and Back (Answer / Explanation).
- **Navigation Controls**: Previous, Next, Shuffle Deck.
- **Page Attribution**: Displays the exact source page where the concept originates.

---

## 3. Leitner Spaced Repetition Progression

Cards progress through 4 mastery levels:
- `Level 0`: Unstudied (New Card)
- `Level 1`: Needs Review (Marked as Difficult)
- `Level 2`: In Progress (Answered correctly once)
- `Level 3`: Mastered (Retained across multiple reviews)

The analytics dashboard tracks total vs mastered cards, feeding directly into the **"Continue Studying"** recommendation.
